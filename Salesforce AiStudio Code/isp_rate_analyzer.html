Triage Classification Rules

GREEN Reusable: Address match, identical product family (DIA vs. EVPL), matching bandwidth, and quoted within the reusable window.

ORANGE Review: Matched location, but requires attention due to stale quote age (13–24 mo), speed tier difference, product mismatch, or address variation.

RED Re-quote / Budgetary: No direct usable quote — net-new site or historical record exceeds the 24-month archival limit (retained as a budgetary baseline).
